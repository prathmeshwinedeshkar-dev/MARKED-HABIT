Privacy

Marked is a personal project. This page says exactly what the app does with your information.

What it stores

  • Your name, college, branch and year, and your "why" line, which you type when making your ID.
  • Your habits, the days they repeat on, and the dates and hours you ticked them.
  • Your light or dark theme choice.

Where it is stored

Only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. There is no account, no server and no database. The developer cannot see your data.

What it does not do

  • No analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • No third-party requests: the font is hosted with the app.
  • No location, contacts or camera access.

Your control

  • Backup and Restore on the My ID tab move your data as a file you hold.
  • Delete all my data on the My ID tab erases everything from this device. Clearing your browser data does the same.
  • Your ID image is created on your device. If you download and share it, it shows your name, so share it only if you want to.

If this changes

If accounts or cloud sync are ever added, this page will be updated first and will ask for your consent before anything leaves your device.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
